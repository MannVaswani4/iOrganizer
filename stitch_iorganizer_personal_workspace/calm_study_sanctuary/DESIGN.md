## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for busy students seeking cognitive relief, systematic structure, and personal privacy. It balances quiet warmth with uncompromising utilitarian clarity. The aesthetic combines intentional minimalism with tactile warmth—replacing sterile, high-stress academic interfaces with a dependable, quiet digital workbench.

Key personality traits:
- **Calm & Grounded:** Generous breathing room, muted warm tones, and zero sensory overload.
- **Focused & Rigorous:** High contrast where legibility matters; crisp scannability across dense assignment trackers and calendars.
- **Warmly Tactile:** Subtle borders, refined card elevations, and tactile surface separations reminiscent of high-end stationery and notebooks.
- **Private & Safe:** Explicit, persistent cues indicating local-first storage, offline resilience, and zero data-mining.

## Colors

The palette employs a calming warm stone canvas paired with crisp slate ink and thoughtful functional accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Warm Stone).
  - Elevated surfaces & cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White) and `#F8FAFC` (Subtle Warm Tint).
  - Secondary containers & muted wells: `#F1F5F9`.
  - Hairline borders & dividers: `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`.

- **Typography & High-Contrast Ink:**
  - Primary text / Headings: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Charcoal).
  - Secondary text / Body: `#334155` (Balanced Slate).
  - Tertiary / Captions / Dimmed labels: `#64748B` (Muted Slate).

- **Intentional Accents:**
  - **Primary (`#0D9488` - Deep Balanced Teal):** Action states, completed marks, steady focus indicators, and primary navigation active tabs.
  - **Secondary (`#4F46E5` - Deep Indigo):** Time-sensitive alerts, critical academic milestones, exam highlights, and interactive toggles.
  - **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber):** "Due Soon", cautionary alerts, and prioritization highlights.
  - **Success / Private (`#059669` - Sage Emerald):** Local sync confirmation, encrypted storage badges, and submitted tasks.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typography levels for its friendly humanistic geometry and open counters, ensuring maximum legibility during late-night study sessions and fast schedule scans.

- **Headlines:** Use slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) to unify word shapes and establish a crisp visual anchor.
- **Body:** Standard letter-spacing with comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.625`) to prevent optical fatigue across syllabi, project briefs, and reading notes.
- **Labels & Chips:** Semi-bold to bold weight with slightly expanded letter-spacing for instant visual classification at tiny sizes (e.g., status badges, due dates, course numbers).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible, responsive 12-column system centered around task orientation:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, max container width of 1280px with centered auto margins. Standard 3-column split: Persistent Slim Sidebar (navigation, calendar mini-picker, privacy state), Primary Stream (agenda, tasks, active course view), and Secondary Inspector (details, subtasks, notes).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, collapsible sidebar transforming into an icon rail or sheet drawer. Main and detail panes toggle or collapse gracefully.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, single-column vertical stack with a bottom sticky navigation bar for core views (Today, Courses, Planner, Settings).

Spacing follows a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm to maintain predictable internal component harmony. Outer containers utilize comfortable margins to maintain an uncluttered desk feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on delicate tonal layering reinforced by ultra-soft, warm ambient shadows and crisp hairline borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FAF9F6`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards / Interactive Widgets):** Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Modals):** White surface with shadow `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Inset (Search bars, input wells, code/note blocks):** Background `#F1F5F9` with a subtle recessed border `#E2E8F0` to signal receptive entry points.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a clean, approachable curvature (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 0.5rem (`8px`) border radius.
- **Container Surfaces (Task Cards, Kanban Columns, Schedule blocks):** `rounded-lg` at 1rem (`16px`).
- **Modals & Flyouts:** `rounded-xl` at 1.5rem (`24px`).
- **Tag Chips & Status Pills:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for quick tactile distinction from rectangular card containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0D9488` with white text. Hover: `#0F766E`. Active: `#115E59`. Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px ring in `#0D9488`.
- **Secondary:** Filled `#F1F5F9` with `#334155` text and 1px border `#E2E8F0`. Hover: `#E2E8F0` background and `#0F172A` text.
- **Ghost / Subtle:** Transparent background, `#475569` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Soft background `#FEF2F2`, `#DC2626` text, border `#FEE2E2`. Hover: `#FEE2E2`.

### Cards & Task Rows
- **Card Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-lg` (16px), Level 1 shadow.
- **Task List Item:** Minimalist row with 0.5rem padding, dividing hairline border `#F1F5F9`. Hover displays quick actions (reschedule, tag, delete) without layout shifting.

### Chips & Tags
- Used for academic categorization (Coursework, Lab, Club, Personal, Exam).
- Built with full pill radius, compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`), and light tinted backgrounds with dark matching text:
  - *Coursework:* `#EEF2FF` background, `#4338CA` text (Indigo).
  - *Lab:* `#F0FDFA` background, `#0F766E` text (Teal).
  - *Exam:* `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text (Red/Coral).
  - *Club:* `#FAF5FF` background, `#7E22CE` text (Purple).
  - *Personal:* `#F8FAFC` background, `#475569` text (Slate).

### Status Badges
- High-contrast dot indicator (6px circle) paired with uppercase `label-sm` text:
  - *Due Soon:* Amber `#D97706` indicator, `#FFFBEB` pill.
  - *In Progress:* Indigo `#4F46E5` indicator, `#EEF2FF` pill.
  - *Completed / Done:* Teal `#0D9488` indicator, `#F0FDFA` pill.
  - *Pending:* Slate `#64748B` indicator, `#F1F5F9` pill.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background (or `#F8FAFC` inside dense tables), `#E2E8F0` 1px border, 8px radius. Active focus transitions border to `#0D9488` with soft teal outer glow.
- **Checkboxes:** Custom rounded square (4px radius). Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Checked: solid `#0D9488` with crisp white checkmark vector. Strikethrough on associated label with 50% opacity slate text.

### Privacy & Local-First Indicators
- **Local Storage Indicator:** Embedded persistent status chip in header or footer: glowing emerald dot (`#059669`) with text *"Encrypted on device / Private"*. Hover reveals backup timestamp and offline readiness status.
